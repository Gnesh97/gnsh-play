## [Unreleased]

### Fixed
- Fixed unbounded Direct3D 12 pipeline latency when the GPU lacks `D3D12VA` Tier 1 decode support (e.g. AMD RX 5500 XT): caught silent FFmpeg software pixel-format fallback on first decoded frame and stepped `D3D12ZeroCopy` through `D3D11Transfer` hardware decode before multi-threaded software decode in [`crates/gnsh-media/src/video_decoder.rs`](crates/gnsh-media/src/video_decoder.rs), added direct semi-planar `NV12`/`P010` GPU plane upload and shader sampling without CPU unpacking in [`crates/gnsh-media/src/d3d12_renderer.rs`](crates/gnsh-media/src/d3d12_renderer.rs), and reported the active decoder path accurately (`D3D12VA` / `D3D11VA` / `SW`) in [`crates/gnsh-app/src/ui/hud.rs`](crates/gnsh-app/src/ui/hud.rs).
- Fixed Vulkan (`libplacebo`) washed/grainy SDR colors, ignored `CAS` sharpness slider, and elevated `Pipe` latency in [`crates/gnsh-media/src/vulkan_renderer.rs`](crates/gnsh-media/src/vulkan_renderer.rs) and [`crates/gnsh-app/src/ui/settings.rs`](crates/gnsh-app/src/ui/settings.rs): mapped SDR video frames to `pl_color_space_srgb`, removed `pl_deband_default_params`, switched to `pl_render_fast_params` (`Bilinear` / `Spline36`) with a dynamic 5-tap `CAS_HOOK_GLSL` mpv user-shader (`pl_mpv_user_shader_parse`), and lowered `swapchain_depth` to `1`.
